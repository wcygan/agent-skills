# Investigation

Explain or diagnose without changing the product.

1. Bound the question and evidence sources. Follow [investigate](../../wcygan-investigate/SKILL.md).
2. Trace observed behavior and historical constraints. Resolve facts through evidence;
   reserve questions for decisions the user owns.
3. Distinguish confirmed findings, inference, and missing evidence. Give a concrete
   next action when the answer reveals further work.

Complete with a cited answer. A diagnosis-only request stays read-only; continue
an already-requested fix from these findings without asking for the same authority again.
