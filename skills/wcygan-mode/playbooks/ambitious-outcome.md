# Ambitious outcome

Choose, enable, or pursue a valuable outcome at the requested level of ownership.

1. If the outcome is undecided, use
   [discover-ambitious-goals](../../discover-ambitious-goals/SKILL.md). A request for
   ideas ends with ranked opportunities; do not pick a new product direction on
   the user's behalf. Continue when the objective is already selected or delegated.
2. If inputs, execution, verification, or authority are uncertain, use
   [prepare-agent-objective](../../prepare-agent-objective/SKILL.md). Reuse an
   existing tested contract. Preparation-only work ends with readiness and gaps.
3. For a bounded improvement, follow the matching feature, bug-fix, or experiment
   playbook. For a stretch target, use
   [experiment](../../wcygan-experiment/SKILL.md) with a fixed acceptance contract.
4. For sustained outcomes, use [maintain-outcome](../../maintain-outcome/SKILL.md)
   for a bounded cycle and checkpoint. Future runs require an authorized, working
   trigger; record its actual status and stop mechanism.
5. Compare results with required and stretch targets separately. Report observed
   scope, evidence, operational confirmation still pending, and the next useful move.

Complete at the requested boundary: opportunities, tested readiness, a verified
improvement, or an evidenced maintenance cycle. Preserve authorization across phases
without treating an aspiration as unlimited resources or permission to publish.
