Gossip protocols

Infection spreads between neighbors

↔ Scroll the diagram to see the complete figure.

Each turn, infected cells contact their four immediate neighbors with a 50% chance of transmission. A cell becomes infected only when its packet arrives; it can spread on the next turn. No diagonal contacts or same-turn cascades. This is an epidemic metaphor, not a disease forecast.

Peers share news about a new member

↔ Scroll the diagram to see the complete figure.

E joins through seed A. A shares the news with B, B with C, then A with D. A node gains the E badge only on receipt. Dashed links are peer relationships; the solid arrow is the current exchange. This illustrates dissemination, not Cassandra’s full wire protocol.

E joins through A. Peers pass the news onward until every member knows E; the seed does not contact everyone itself.
