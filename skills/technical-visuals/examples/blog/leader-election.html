Follow the evidence

One vote per term

Each node records its term and vote. A candidate counts its own vote, but still needs two peers. Delivered votes never carry into a new term.

A split is not a winner

In the split schedule, A and C time out together and each receives two votes. Different retry timers eventually let one candidate collect three votes in a later term.

Recovery changes the role

The crashed node returns after 2.5 model seconds (five seconds of playback). Its durable term and vote survive; it rejoins as a follower and learns the current leader from messages.

Coverage: the original RaftElectionDemo, including both schedules, repeated leader crashes, recovery, terms, votes, timers, and traveling messages. Deterministic teaching timings; no log replication, log freshness comparison, or network partition control. The majority remains three of five even during a crash.
