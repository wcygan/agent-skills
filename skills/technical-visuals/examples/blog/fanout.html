Fanout

One request reaches many services

Each middle service calls two back ends. Back end 3 receives five calls for every one on the other paths in this illustrative burst. Responses are omitted.

One request creates six reads

Each request creates three downstream calls, each with two reads. At 5 incoming requests/s, this model produces 15 calls/s and 30 reads/s; playback is slowed down.

Removing unused calls removes work

The post reports a drop from roughly 8,000 to 10 downstream queries/s after redundant calls were removed. This two-day curve and deployment time are illustrative, not telemetry.
