Distributed transactions

Participants prepare before the coordinator commits

Explore rejection or unavailable delivery

↔ Scroll the diagram to see both participants

The coordinator commits only after every participant votes YES. Prepared participants hold their locks until the decision arrives; the demo finishes after both acknowledgements return.

Each shard needs its own majority

↔ Scroll the diagram to see both shard groups

Each shard needs two of its three copies. The transaction needs every shard to prepare, then each shard leader acknowledges the shared decision.

Two-phase commit coordinates one atomic outcome across participants. Phase one completes after vote replies arrive. Phase two saves and delivers the decision and receives acknowledgements. Prepared YES voters retain row locks; a timeout cannot safely erase their promise. This is different from two-phase locking, which governs when a transaction acquires and releases locks.
