DESIGN SYSTEM

Quiet surfaces. Specific emphasis.

Purple identifies processing, blue storage, teal flow, and rose control. Orange highlights the selected teaching point. Status colors accompany explicit labels. Shape and text carry meaning without color. Theme and camera changes preserve the selected state.
