A few things to know.

What can I try in the demo?

Name a sample workspace, select a plan, and update three sample tasks. Their states stay in this browser when you reopen the demo. Team collaboration and the full feature list are product concepts.

Do I need to sign up?

No. This standalone demo does not create an account, request an email address, or connect to a team service.

Will selecting Team charge me?

No. Selecting a plan changes only the label in your sample workspace. Prices and plan limits are illustrative and no payment is collected.

Where is my workspace saved?

In this browser’s local storage, separately from the other Forma demos. If browser storage is unavailable, you can still explore the workspace for this session; changes will not persist.
