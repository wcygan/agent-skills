A log remembers every change.

Records append at the tail with increasing log sequence numbers (LSNs). Updating a key adds a record; earlier records remain in the log.

ORDERLSNFRAMINGkey byte lengthFRAMINGvalue byte lengthPAYLOADkey bytesPAYLOADvalue bytes

Lengths tell a binary reader how many bytes to consume for each payload. These are UTF-8 byte counts, not character counts. The layout is illustrative: real logs may add record types, checksums, transaction markers, and version headers; their LSN may identify a byte position rather than a record number.
